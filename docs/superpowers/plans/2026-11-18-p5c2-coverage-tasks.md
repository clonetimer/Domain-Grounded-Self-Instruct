## Review Focus

- Stable received carrier is not proof that every RF quantity is healthy or that one diode failed.
- Different test conditions may explain two valid observations; the unsupported generalization is the conflict.
- Co-varying elapsed time and thermal cycles do not separate competing causal explanations.
- Missing post-restart baseline proves neither recovery nor continuing abnormality.
- Invalid wheel/window attribution supports an input-quality conclusion, not hardware health or failure.
