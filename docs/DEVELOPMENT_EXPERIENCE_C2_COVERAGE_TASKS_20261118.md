# Development experience: P5-C2 coverage tasks (2026-11-18)

Continuation of lessons 115–120 in [the preceding experience record](DEVELOPMENT_EXPERIENCE_TOPOLOGY_REVIEW.md). These lessons concern the six additional literature-derived candidates documented in [the coverage report](P5C2_COVERAGE_TASKS_2026-11-18.md). Scientific and source-rights admission remain pending; engineering evidence is recorded separately in the preflight and delivery receipt.

121. **Identify the disputed proposition before calling observations contradictory.** A falling onboard power indication and a stable received carrier can coexist because they measure different quantities through different chains. In candidate 016, the disputed claim is the ML inference of a comparable transmitted-carrier decrease. Compare equipment identity, time window, baseline, carrier, mode and the supplied quality checks before weighing that inference. Preserve both observations when their scopes permit coexistence. Evidence against the ML interpretation does not, by itself, prove a particular monitor diode has failed or that total RF output is healthy.

122. **A condition-specific PASS does not erase a condition-specific FAIL.** In candidate 017, the same identified slip ring has different reported outcomes in low- and high-humidity air tests. Retain both results with their conditions; challenge the unsupported conclusion that the later PASS establishes normal behavior across the relevant qualification range. Record which identities and declared profiles match and which facility, acquisition-chain, handling or sequence differences remain. Do not make humidity the sole cause merely because it is a conspicuous difference. Keep these air tests distinct from the historical investigation's initial TVAC event and from orbital qualification.

123. **Separate synthetic identity from the strength of each inference inside the scenario.** Mark current observations, comparison checks, scores and identifiers as synthetic at the model-visible boundary. Then use the supplied evidence according to its declared scope. A synthetic independent comparison can be sufficient to reject an overbroad synthetic ML claim while remaining insufficient to locate a physical root cause. Do not discard a supplied check as missing merely because it is synthetic, and do not convert a scenario-quality check into actual mission evidence or expert endorsement. Rank uncertainty by the unresolved claim, not by a blanket rule that every synthetic input is maximally uncertain. Keep the input factual: review of 017 found a sentence prescribing how to write the conclusion inside a diagnostic log; moving it to curator-only review preserved the evidence while removing the answer hint.

124. **A justified abstention must preserve what is known and request the smallest discriminating summary.** Withholding a unique startup mechanism does not erase the observed trend when elapsed time and thermal cycling co-vary. Withholding hydrogen-poisoning attribution does not erase whole-unit power loss. Withholding proven recovery after a restart does not establish persistent failure either. State the unsupported proposition, the narrower supported observation and the evidence that could change the decision. Bind each assertion to its actual supporting record: the 020 executed-action field was narrowed to the restart supported by its event log, while the later trend and recovery limits remain in their analysis fields. Request upstream summaries of the relevant controlled comparison, component isolation, or matched healthy baseline and follow-up window; do not default to raw telemetry, repeat already supplied checks, or demand every possible inspection.

125. **An input-binding error establishes neither hardware health nor hardware failure.** A wheel or time-window mismatch makes the affected ML conclusion unusable for the intended current case. Treat that as an inference-quality limitation, not as a normal result, a physical fault diagnosis or proof that all available evidence is invalid. Identify which binding failed and which observations remain attributable. Ask for a corrected device/window association and the resulting validated upstream summary before reconsidering the hardware claim. A repaired input pipeline and a repaired spacecraft are different outcomes.

126. **Count additional decision tasks separately from independent incidents, and change only the deliverables that need to change.** The six additions extend conflict and evidence-sufficiency coverage within existing source families; they do not create six independent flight or ground-test events. Keep the corrected original 15 packets canonical-identical, retain the existing L0 bytes and preserve event-family grouping. Existing instruction templates and the projected task type can express these tasks without a schema or runtime expansion. Version the expanded cohort and its review bindings independently, reuse RC4, and place new lessons in a separate companion document when editing a packaged document would otherwise change the archive bytes. More candidate rows are evidence of broader task coverage, not new expert-approved or training-authorized samples.
