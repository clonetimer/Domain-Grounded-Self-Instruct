# Development experience after the C2 preliminary review return

Continuation of [lessons 121–126](DEVELOPMENT_EXPERIENCE_C2_COVERAGE_TASKS_20261118.md). The concrete evidence and dispositions are in the [review response](P5C2_AI_PRE_REVIEW_RESPONSE_2026-11-18.md) and its linked machine-readable ledgers.

127. **Matching metadata does not prove that the bound content was reviewed.** The returned advice carries all 15 original packet and source hashes, yet its author explicitly lacked the complete frozen inputs. Validate archive integrity, cohort identity, material availability and reviewer identity as separate facts. A correct hash can identify an intended object without proving that its body was examined. Two perspectives from one AI system are useful hypotheses, not two authenticated independent reviewers.

128. **Reconcile old advice with current evidence before changing a corrected candidate.** All 15 returned packet/source bindings differ from the current versions; all corrected baseline packets match their counterparts in the expanded cohort. Map a useful concern to the current fields and preserve the old review's provenance. Do not transfer scores or decisions across cohorts, and do not create a new data version just to demonstrate activity when the current content already addresses the issue. A rejected envelope and a separately proven hash mismatch are different observations; report the actual first rejection accurately.

129. **Test a proposed correction's premise.** The conditional sampling concern for 010 must be checked against its supplied chemical result in `LOG-A2`. In 011, the current input also supplies component-isolation and gas/EMMI findings; broader review concerns must not be extended into a demand to erase that evidence. The return did not explicitly request that downgrade for 011. Evaluate the strength and limits of the supplied scenario evidence without presenting it as a real experiment. Synthetic provenance is a disclosure boundary, not a rule requiring every conclusion to have maximal uncertainty. Likewise, a declared machine-evidence ID is not dangling merely because a reviewer expects another schema, and raw telemetry is not required by a task explicitly designed around upstream summaries.

130. **Preserve equipment, chronology and decision scope when interpreting review language.** TWTA power indication is not an EPS power-supply diagnosis; helix current is not a mechanical helix actuator. Keep mission-specific A/B outcomes, the two FUSE wheel episodes, ground investigation and flight operation, historical success and current recovery, and advice versus executed action distinct. Compare publication availability to the declared synthetic analysis cutoff, not to a substituted historical mission date. A context-only historical case with unknown hardware compatibility cannot be promoted into a diagnostic vote.

131. **Rights dependencies include context and aliases, but permission still requires applicable evidence.** The returned register used four alternate identifiers and omitted Cassini's historical dependency in task 012. Link aliases by the known official record, report number or DOI and enumerate every current dependency, including newer task variants. Keep the authoritative queue unchanged until actual decisions arrive. Public distribution, a Government-use label, general publisher policy and an AI note hash represent different evidence. A hash authenticates bytes, not a rights holder or a reuse permission; a different DOI's policy fragment cannot serve as the target article's license.

132. **A review handoff must expose the complete current material and the remaining work.** Supply the full scientific dossier, exact L0/L1 snapshots and fresh current forms where the reviewer can find them directly. State the six tasks absent from the returned advice. Keep pending expert completion, source-rights review and authorized SFT output visible after incorporating useful feedback. Add derived response records and a readable disposition report while preserving immutable task, form and runtime files. This advances review readiness without manufacturing scientific acceptance or increasing the training count.
